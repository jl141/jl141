Nullwood - Infinite Platformer - Java Programming

Summer 2021
  • "You meet a mysterious twerp at a pub, who tells you of an old legend... There is said to be a sacred treasure of otherworldy riches held deep inside Nullwood. You, being a skilled warrior who has mastered magic and swordfighting, are unafraid to venture into the unknowns of Nullwood to claim the treasure..."
  • Player run and jump from platform to platform, slashing their sword and casting offensive spells to defeat enemies in their path.
  • The first platformer I made was cheesable: holding the space bar would prevent the small slime enemies from being able to charge their bite attack. This means the player could hold the space bar until all slimes have spawned, then kill them all at once with a couple AOE sword slashes.
  • I made sure Nullwood wouldn't be cheesable: the player can run past enemies without getting hit by a melee attack, but overtaken enemies would begin launching offensive spells of their own, making it impossible for the player to get far without being overwhelmed by a wall of spellshots.
  • Written in Java, developed in the Greenfoot IDE.
  • "Animated Pixel Adventurer" sprites from rvros on itch.io: https://rvros.itch.io/
